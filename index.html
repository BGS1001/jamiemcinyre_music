About

Jamie McIntyre is a singer-songwriter from Galway, writing modern folk that pairs acoustic warmth with honest, heartfelt storytelling.

He started out busking on the streets of his home city. When “Over Galway Town” took off online, it carried his voice from the West of Ireland to tens of millions of listeners — and his debut Irish tour sold out in Dublin, Limerick and Galway.

Since then he has played Electric Picnic, supported Jamie Webster, and was hand-picked by Gavin James to open for him in Paris. His EP For Folk Sake arrived in September 2025.
